# IEEE Grid GNN Experiments

All samples are synthetic pandapower AC power-flow solutions. The data generator samples valid non-islanding single-branch outages and a separate high-load stress mixture (load scale 1.3–1.6; ordinary sampling 0.6–1.4). The supervised metric is evaluated on unknown bus voltages in a random held-out split.

## Interpretation and limits

The voltage-violation counts describe operating points generated by the power-flow solver; they are not evidence that the GNN predicts or corrects violations. Baseline metrics summarize one seeded random split per grid. The topology-transfer sweep is the direct evidence for generalization across grid structures: errors trend down as the fine-tuning set grows, with a small non-monotonic step at 10 samples. The AC mismatch term is used during masked pretraining, while the reported supervised baseline uses state labels. IEEE 118 has a maximum stored-state mismatch of 2.19e-4 p.u. under the differentiable residual check, much larger than the other cases; this deserves investigation before interpreting physics-pretraining results. Synthetic benchmark results do not establish performance on field networks or operational safety.
